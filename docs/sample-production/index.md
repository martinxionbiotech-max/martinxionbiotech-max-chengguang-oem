# OEM Sample Production

## What to Expect

Sample production is the critical step between specification and mass production.

### Timeline: 3-4 weeks

| Week | Activity |
|------|----------|
| Week 1 | Label artwork finalization, production scheduling |
| Week 2 | Battery assembly with your specifications |
| Week 3 | Formation charging, testing, labeling |
| Week 4 | Documentation, packaging, shipment |

### What You Receive

- 5-20 production samples with your brand
- Certificate of Analysis (CCA, capacity, voltage per sample)
- Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS)
- Label and packaging approval confirmation

### Sample Cost

Sample costs are credited against your first production order. [Contact for sample pricing](https://chengguangenergy.com/contact/).

### After Samples Arrive

1. Test in your target vehicles and climate conditions
2. Verify specifications match the CoA
3. Approve label quality and packaging
4. Confirm or adjust specifications
5. Place production order
