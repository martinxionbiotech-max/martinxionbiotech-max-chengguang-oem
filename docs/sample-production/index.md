# Sample Production — Complete Guide

Sample production is the critical bridge between specification and mass production — your opportunity to test the product in your market before committing to a full container order.

## Sample Timeline: 3-4 Weeks

| Week | Activity | What You Receive |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| **Week 1** | Label artwork finalization, production slot scheduling | Label proof for approval |
| **Week 2** | Battery assembly with your specifications (label, terminal, packaging) | Production progress update |
| **Week 3** | Formation charging, 100% testing, quality inspection | Test data preview |
| **Week 4** | Documentation, export packaging, courier shipment | Physical samples + documents |

## What You Receive

- **5-20 branded production samples** — exactly as mass production will deliver
- **Certificate of Analysis per sample** — CCA, capacity (C20), voltage, visual inspection
- **MSDS** (Material Safety Data Sheet) — for import customs clearance
- **Label and packaging approval confirmation**
- **Shipping tracking number**

## Sample Cost

Sample costs (battery + courier) are **credited against your first production order**. This means samples are effectively free once you place your production order.

## What to Test

1. **CCA** — verify with your own tester (compare to CoA values)
2. **Capacity** — test in your climate conditions
3. **Fitment** — install in target vehicles, verify terminal fit
4. **Label durability** — expose to heat, moisture, chemicals
5. **Packaging integrity** — check after courier transport
6. **Market acceptance** — show to your customers for feedback
