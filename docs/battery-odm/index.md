# ODM Battery Manufacturing

ODM (Original Design Manufacturing) — Chengguang designs and manufactures a battery to your unique specification. For when off-the-shelf models don't meet your market requirement.

## The OEM Funnel

**Your Input:** Unique specification requirements (dimensions, capacity, CCA, terminal)
**Chengguang Responsibility:** Engineering design, prototyping, testing, production
**Deliverable:** Custom-designed battery prototype
**Quality Checkpoint:** Specification verification, performance testing

## Key Considerations

- **MOQ**: 200-500 units/model for label-only; 2,000+ for full custom
- **Lead Time**: 3-4 weeks for samples; 4-8 weeks for mass production
- **Standards**: JIS, DIN, BCI — we manufacture to all major standards
- **Certification**: IATF 16949, ISO 9001, ISO 45001, CE
