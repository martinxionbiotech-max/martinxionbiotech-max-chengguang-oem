# Battery Packaging

Packaging is your brand's first physical impression. Chengguang offers fully customized packaging solutions from individual cartons to pallet-level branding.

## Packaging Options

| Level | Description | MOQ Impact |
|-------|-------------|:---:|
| **Standard** | Plain brown carton with model label | Included |
| **Branded carton** | Your logo/colors on box | +500/model |
| **Retail-ready** | Full-color box with product photography, features, multilingual text | +1,000/model |
| **Pallet wrapping** | Branded stretch film, corner protectors | Per order |
| **Display packaging** | Shelf-ready retail packaging | +2,000/model |

## What Goes in the Box

- Battery with your brand label
- Warranty card (your terms, your language)
- User manual / installation guide (multilingual available)
- Terminal protectors
- Handling instructions

## Export Packaging

All Chengguang export packaging is **DG-compliant** for UN 2794 (Batteries, wet, filled with acid, Class 8). Packaging meets IATA/IMDG/ADR dangerous goods transport requirements. Palletized with acid-proof lining and spill containment.
