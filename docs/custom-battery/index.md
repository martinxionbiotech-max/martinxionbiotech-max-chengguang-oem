# Custom Battery Manufacturing

Full custom battery manufacturing — when standard models don't meet your market's unique requirements. We engineer a battery to your exact specification: case dimensions, capacity, CCA, terminal type, plate design.

## What Can Be Customized

| Element | Options | Impact |
|---------|---------|--------|
| **Case dimensions** | Custom LxWxH (within production capability) | Unique market positioning |
| **Capacity (Ah)** | Tuned via plate count/paste density | Match competitor or exceed |
| **CCA** | Tuned via grid design/alloy | Cold climate optimization |
| **Terminal type** | T1 small/large, DIN, SAE, custom | Regional standard compliance |
| **Terminal position** | L-/R+, L+/R-, custom | Fit specific vehicle models |
| **Case color** | Any RAL color | Brand differentiation |
| **Plate design** | Thickness, grid pattern | Deep cycle vs. high CCA optimization |

## The Custom Process

1. **Specification:** You describe your requirement (or send a competitor sample)
2. **Feasibility:** Chengguang engineering assesses manufacturability (1-2 weeks)
3. **Prototype:** 10-20 custom samples produced (4-6 weeks)
4. **Testing:** You test in your market conditions
5. **Production:** Full production after prototype approval
