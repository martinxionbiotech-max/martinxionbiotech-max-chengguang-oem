# EFB battery OEM

Chengguang Power Tech manufactures EFB (enhanced flooded) batteries on an OEM basis for entry-level start-stop and higher-cycling applications.

## What EFB means

EFB is an enhanced flooded battery: conventional flooded construction with improvements that raise cycling durability. It sits between standard SLI and AGM — better cycling than SLI, lower cost than AGM.

## Where EFB fits

Entry-level start-stop vehicles, vehicles with moderate accessory load, and markets where AGM cost is not justified by the duty cycle.

## What you define

Standard family and case (JIS, DIN/EN, BCI), capacity at C20, CCA on a named test basis, terminal type and polarity, and the label and packaging.

## OEM terms

MOQ follows the container (typically one 20ft container); production lead time is 30–45 days. Manufacturing is under IATF 16949 and ISO 9001 with 100% end-of-line testing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is EFB better than a standard SLI battery?

For start-stop duty, yes — EFB is built for more cycling than a standard SLI battery. It is not a substitute for AGM where the vehicle specifies AGM.

### Can EFB batteries be private-labelled?

Yes. EFB batteries are available for private-label programs with your brand, label, packaging and documentation.

### What MOQ applies to EFB OEM?

Typically one 20ft container; the exact load depends on group size and pallet configuration.

### Which markets use EFB?

Mainly European and other markets with widespread entry-level start-stop vehicles.
