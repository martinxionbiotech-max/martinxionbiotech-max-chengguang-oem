## Stage Details

### 1. Inquiry & Proposal
**Your Input:** Target market, vehicle types, preferred standard (JIS/DIN/BCI), target price point, annual volume estimate
**Chengguang Deliverable:** OEM proposal with recommended models, MOQ, ballpark pricing
**Key Resource:** [:material-domain: Main Site — About Our Factory](https://chengguangenergy.com/about/)

### 2. Specification Matching
**Your Input:** Selected model, customization requirements
**Chengguang Deliverable:** Technical specification sheet, label layout proof
**Key Resource:** [:material-car-battery: Battery Database — All 14 Models](https://data.chengguangenergy.com/battery-models/)

### 3. Technical Review
**Your Input:** Engineering questions about performance, durability, regional suitability
**Chengguang Deliverable:** Technical consultation with manufacturing engineers
**Key Resource:** [:material-bookshelf: Technical Center — Technology Deep Dives](https://technical.chengguangenergy.com/)

### 4. Sample Production
**Chengguang Deliverable:** 5-20 branded production samples with CoA
**Timeline:** 3-4 weeks from specification sign-off
**Key Resource:** [:material-test-tube: Sample Production Guide](sample-production.md)

### 5. Mass Production
**Chengguang Deliverable:** Full production batch with IATF 16949 traceability
**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks after sample approval
**Key Resource:** [:material-factory: Factory Tour & Capabilities](https://chengguangenergy.com/about/)

### 6. Pre-Shipment Inspection
**Chengguang Deliverable:** Inspection report, CoA, MSDS, DG declaration
**Key Resource:** [:material-certificate: Quality Certifications](https://chengguangenergy.com/quality-certifications/)

### 7. Shipment
**Chengguang Deliverable:** DG-compliant export packaging (UN 2794 / Class 8)
**Key Resource:** [:material-earth: Market Intelligence — Your Destination](https://market.chengguangenergy.com/regions/)
