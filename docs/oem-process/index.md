# OEM Battery Manufacturing Process

From initial inquiry to container shipment — the complete OEM journey with Chengguang.

---

## The Complete Funnel

| Stage | Duration | What Happens | Your Role |
|-------|:--:|-------------|-----------|
| **1. Inquiry** | 1-3 days | Submit requirements, receive proposal | Describe your market need |
| **2. Specification** | 1-2 weeks | Match battery model, define custom specs | Review and confirm |
| **3. Sample** | 3-4 weeks | Produce branded samples | Approve samples |
| **4. Production** | 4-8 weeks | Mass production under your brand | Monitor progress |
| **5. Inspection** | 3-5 days | Pre-shipment QC, documentation | Approve shipment |
| **6. Shipment** | 2-6 weeks | DG-compliant export packaging | Receive goods |

---

## Stage Details

### 1. Inquiry & Proposal
**Customer Input:** Market, vehicle types, preferred standard (JIS/DIN/BCI), target price point
**Chengguang Deliverable:** OEM proposal with recommended battery models, MOQ, ballpark pricing
**Quality Checkpoint:** Specification feasibility confirmed

### 2. Specification Matching
**Customer Input:** Selected model, customization requirements (label, terminal, packaging)
**Chengguang Deliverable:** Technical specification sheet, label layout proof, packaging design
**Quality Checkpoint:** Technical sign-off by both parties

### 3. Sample Production
**Chengguang Deliverable:** 5-20 branded production samples with CoA documentation
**Quality Checkpoint:** CCA, capacity, voltage, visual inspection

### 4. Mass Production
**Chengguang Deliverable:** Full production batch with batch traceability
**Quality Checkpoint:** In-process QC at each manufacturing stage

### 5. Pre-Shipment Inspection
**Chengguang Deliverable:** Inspection report, CoA, MSDS, DG declaration, packing list
**Quality Checkpoint:** Third-party inspection (SGS/BV) available on request

### 6. Shipment
**Chengguang Deliverable:** DG-compliant export packaging (UN 2794 / Class 8), shipping documents
**Quality Checkpoint:** Container loading verification
