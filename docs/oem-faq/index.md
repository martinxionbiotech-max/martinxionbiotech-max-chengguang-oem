# OEM Battery Manufacturing FAQ

## General

**Q: What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?**

| Order Type | MOQ |
|-----------|-----|
| Standard product (factory label) | 1x 20ft container (~400-600 batteries) |
| Custom label only | 200-500 per model |
| Custom label + packaging | 500-1,000 per model |
| Full custom (case + terminals + specs) | 2,000-5,000 per model |

LCL (Less than Container Load) available for trial orders.

**Q: How long does OEM production take?**

Samples: 3-4 weeks. Mass production: 4-8 weeks after sample approval. Shipping: 2-6 weeks depending on destination.

**Q: Can I mix battery models in one container?**

Yes. Standard practice — mix SLI, heavy duty, JIS, and DIN models in one shipment.

**Q: What certifications do you have?**

IATF 16949 (automotive quality), ISO 9001 (quality management), ISO 45001 (health & safety), CE marking (EU compliance).

## Customization

**Q: Can I use my own battery case color?**

Yes. Custom case colors available for production orders of 2,000+ units per model.

**Q: Can you match competitor specifications?**

Yes. Send us a competitor battery specification and we will propose an equivalent from our range or develop a custom match.

**Q: What label customization options are available?**

Your brand logo, colors, layout, multi-language text, QR codes, hologram security labels, regulatory markings.

## Payment & Shipping

**Q: What payment terms do you offer?**

T/T (30% deposit, 70% before shipment). L/C at sight available for established relationships.

**Q: Do you handle export documentation?**

Yes. Full export documentation: commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, CoA, MSDS, DG declaration, and any destination-country specific documents.

**Q: Is shipping included in the price?**

FOB Tianjin/Xingang is standard. CIF/CFR available on request.
