# SLI battery OEM

Chenguang Power Tech manufactures SLI (starting, lighting, ignition) batteries on an OEM basis — conventional flooded starting batteries for standard vehicles.

## What SLI means

SLI stands for starting, lighting and ignition: the conventional flooded lead-acid battery that starts the engine and supports the vehicle's electrical load. It is the baseline automotive battery type.

## Where SLI fits

Standard vehicles without start-stop systems, and applications where a simple, cost-effective starting battery is the right specification.

## What you define

Standard family and case (JIS, DIN/EN, BCI), capacity at C20, CCA on a named test basis, terminal type and polarity, and the label and packaging.

## OEM terms

MOQ follows the container (typically one 20ft container); production lead time is 30–45 days. Manufacturing is under IATF 16949 and ISO 9001 with 100% end-of-line testing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does SLI stand for?

Starting, lighting and ignition — the conventional flooded lead-acid automotive battery type.

### Can SLI batteries be supplied under my brand?

Yes. SLI batteries are available for OEM and private-label programs, with specification, sampling, branding and packaging confirmed on inquiry.

### What MOQ applies to SLI OEM?

Typically one 20ft container (~600–800 units), because freight cost per unit collapses at a full container load.

### Which standards are available for SLI?

JIS, DIN/EN, BCI and regional (AS/SA) fitments, confirmed per market.
