# Battery Label Design

Your battery label is your brand's most visible asset — seen every time a customer opens their hood. Chengguang supports full label customization with in-house design review and production.

## Label Elements

| Element | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **Brand logo** | Full color, any size |
| **Brand name** | Your registered brand/trademark |
| **Model designation** | Your naming system or standard model codes |
| **Specifications** | CCA, Ah, voltage — per your market's standard format |
| **Languages** | English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese |
| **Regulatory marks** | CE, WEEE, recycling symbols, country of origin |
| **Barcode** | EAN-13, UPC, or custom |
| **QR code** | Link to warranty registration, product info, or your website |
| **Security** | Hologram, UV ink, tamper-evident seal (on request) |

## Design Process

1. You provide your brand assets (logo, colors, text)
2. Chengguang creates a label layout proof (2-3 days)
3. You review and approve or request revisions
4. Physical label samples produced (1 week)
5. Applied to sample batteries for your final approval

## Label Durability

All Chengguang labels use **UV-resistant lamination** with **chemical-resistant adhesive** rated for under-hood temperatures (-40degC to +100degC) and acid exposure. Labels will not peel, fade, or delaminate during the battery's service life.
