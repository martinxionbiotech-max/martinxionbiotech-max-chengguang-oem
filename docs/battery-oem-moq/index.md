# Battery OEM MOQ

OEM battery MOQ is set by container logistics, not by negotiation alone. For lead-acid starter batteries the binding limit is usually pallet stacking and carton strength, not raw volume or weight.

## Why MOQ follows the container

Shipping cost per unit collapses at a full container load (FCL). For lead-acid batteries the 20ft container is the standard unit — but the practical load is capped well below the container's raw volume or payload by pallet stacking and carton crush strength.

## The three limits

| Limit | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Volume | 20ft container ≈ 33 m³ ÷ ~0.011 m³ per boxed battery | ≈ 3,000 units |
| Payload | ≈ 28,000 kg ÷ ~19 kg per battery | ≈ 1,470 units |
| Stacking / handling | Pallet load limit and carton crush strength | ≈ 600–800 units |

The honest conclusion: for starter batteries the binding constraint is pallet stacking, not raw volume or weight. A pallet of 70 batteries at 19 kg each already weighs about 1,330 kg, near the practical handling limit.

## What you can do

- **Order in full container multiples** — FCL pricing is the single biggest unit-cost lever.
- **Mix models into one container** — spread the fixed freight across more line items.
- **Use LCL for a pilot** — pay a higher per-unit freight on a part-load to validate before committing a full container.

## Lead time

Production lead time is 30–45 days; ocean freight and clearance add 3–5 weeks on top.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is battery MOQ tied to a container?

Freight cost per unit collapses at a full container load, and pallet stacking caps the practical load well below the container's raw volume or payload.

### Can I get a lower MOQ for a first order?

Sometimes — discuss a prototype or pilot batch (or an LCL part-load) before committing to the full production MOQ. Expect the freight per unit to run higher at low volume.

### How many starter batteries fit in a 20ft container?

Roughly 600–800 units once pallets, packaging and stacking limits are accounted for.

### Does lead-acid production have a seasonal cycle?

No. Unlike agricultural products, lead-acid batteries are produced year-round, so lead time is stable rather than seasonal.
