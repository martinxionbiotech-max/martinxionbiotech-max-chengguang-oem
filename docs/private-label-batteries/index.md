# Private Label Battery Manufacturing

Private label is the fastest path to selling batteries under your own brand. You use Chengguang's existing product range (14 models across JIS and DIN) with your brand identity applied. No new tooling, no minimum mold costs, no engineering delays.

## Private Label vs. Full OEM

| | Private Label | Full OEM |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Product** | Standard Chengguang models | Custom-engineered battery |
| **Tooling** | None required | May require new molds |
| **Lead Time** | 3-4 weeks to samples | 8-12 weeks to samples |
| **MOQ** | 200-500/model | 2,000-5,000/model |
| **Cost** | Standard pricing + label fee | Premium + tooling amortization |
| **Best For** | Market entry, brand testing | Unique market positioning |

## What We Brand

- **Top label** — your logo, colors, model designation, specifications, barcode
- **Side labels** — marketing text, features, warranty terms (multi-language)
- **Handle** — custom color
- **Terminal caps** — custom color

## Markets We Serve

Chengguang currently manufactures under **50+ international brand labels** for distributors and importers in Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, South America, and Oceania.
