# Private Label Battery Manufacturing

Private label means you sell batteries under your own brand using Chengguang's existing product range. Faster than full OEM — no tooling or new mold costs.

## The OEM Funnel

**Your Input:** Brand name, target market, label design
**Chengguang Responsibility:** Apply your branding to standard products, quality assurance
**Deliverable:** Branded battery samples (3-4 weeks)
**Quality Checkpoint:** Label quality, terminal configuration, packaging compliance

## Key Considerations

- **MOQ**: 200-500 units/model for label-only; 2,000+ for full custom
- **Lead Time**: 3-4 weeks for samples; 4-8 weeks for mass production
- **Standards**: JIS, DIN, BCI — we manufacture to all major standards
- **Certification**: IATF 16949, ISO 9001, ISO 45001, CE
