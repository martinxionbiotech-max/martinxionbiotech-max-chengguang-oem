## What OEM Means Here

OEM is not just "put my logo on a battery". It is a full manufacturing partnership, from electrical specification to the finished, labelled product in your warehouse. You control the **six levers** that define your brand:

| Lever | What you decide |
|---|---|
| **Model & spec** | JIS or DIN model, Ah, CCA, terminal, reserve capacity |
| **Label** | Your brand, languages, country compliance marks |
| **Case** | Colour, material, handle and lid design |
| **Terminal** | Type, position, polarity layout |
| **Packaging** | Carton printing, pallet configuration |
| **Quality** | Test protocol, batch traceability, certificates |

Because we are **OEM-first** — we do not sell under our own retail brand — we never compete with the customers whose brands we build. That single fact changes the whole relationship: your market stays yours.

### The timeline, in plain terms

- **Specification sign-off** → samples in **3–4 weeks**.
- **Sample approval** → mass production in **4–8 weeks**.
- **Ocean freight** → roughly **2–6 weeks** depending on destination.
- Total: a typical OEM program goes from first call to landed product in about **3–4 months**.

### Minimum order quantities

| Order type | Starting MOQ |
|---|---|
| Factory-label (standard models) | ~1 × 20 ft container (400–600 batteries) |
| Custom label | 200–500 units per model |
| Full custom (case + label + spec) | ~2,000–5,000 units per model |

MOQs vary by model and customization depth — confirm the exact figure for your specification before committing.
