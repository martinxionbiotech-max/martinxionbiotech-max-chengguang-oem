# AGM battery OEM

Chengguang Power Tech manufactures AGM (absorbent glass mat) batteries on an OEM basis for start-stop vehicles and high-electrical-load applications.

## What AGM means

AGM batteries hold the electrolyte in a glass-fibre mat rather than as free liquid. They accept charge faster, cycle deeper and handle higher electrical loads than conventional flooded batteries — which is why start-stop and high-accessory vehicles specify them.

## Where AGM fits

Start-stop cars and light-commercial vehicles, vehicles with high accessory load, and applications where cycling durability matters more than the lowest unit cost.

## What you define

Standard family and case (JIS, DIN/EN, BCI), capacity at C20, CCA on a named test basis, terminal type and polarity, and the label and packaging.

## OEM terms

MOQ follows the container (typically one 20ft container); production lead time is 30–45 days. AGM orders may add sampling time for specification confirmation. Manufacturing is under IATF 16949 and ISO 9001 with 100% end-of-line testing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between AGM and EFB?

AGM uses a glass-fibre mat that holds electrolyte and supports higher charge acceptance; EFB is an enhanced flooded battery with better cycling than a standard SLI but a lower cost point. Start-stop vehicles may specify either, depending on the vehicle.

### Can Chengguang OEM AGM batteries under my brand?

Yes. AGM batteries are available for OEM and private-label programs; specification, sampling, branding and packaging are confirmed on inquiry.

### What MOQ applies to AGM OEM?

Typically one 20ft container, because lead-acid freight economics collapse at a full container load and pallet stacking caps the practical load.

### Which standards are available for AGM?

JIS, DIN/EN and BCI group sizes, confirmed per market.
